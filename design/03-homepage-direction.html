01 / Taxonomy

Two layers, one navigation

You were right that neither set should automatically win. Pandonia already speaks in two vocabularies — it has simply never put them on the same screen. The evidence is in Appendix A §01; this is the UX consequence.

Layer 1 — health areasNavigation · comprehension · scores

Answers "what part of my health am I learning about?" Carries the navigation, the category pages, the six scores, and every count. Written the way a person would search.

Hjerte og karBlodsukker og stofskifteHormoner AfventerAfventerAfventer

Three from your concept. The other three are Pandonia's to name, not mine to invent. Appendix A lists eight health-area terms Pandonia already uses in its own commercial copy — evidence, not a proposal.

Layer 2 — the Pandonia modelContext · never navigation

Answers "why do these markers belong together?" Appears as a mono annotation on a category page, on a marker detail, and as a full explainer on Om os. It is an attribute, not a folder.

SIGNALERINGTRANSPORTFORDØJELSE ENERGIIMMUNFORSVARAFFALDSSYSTEM

Verified verbatim from the live site, both languages. Two of these six are flagged for review — see Appendix A §03.

Why this is the right answer, and what it costs

It optimises comprehension without losing the science

A visitor who wants to know about their cholesterol finds "Hjerte og kar" and is done. A visitor who wants to know why cholesterol sits there finds one mono line saying it is a transport marker — and, if they follow it, the whole model. Neither reader is made to work for the other.

The biological model is the ownable half

Every competitor organises by organ. Nobody organises by what the body does. Demoting Layer 2 to navigation would throw away the only structural idea Pandonia already owns — but promoting it to navigation would make "where is my cholesterol?" unanswerable. Context is exactly the right altitude for it.

The constraint: it is not a hierarchy

A marker can be one health area and one system, and they cut across each other. Many-to-many, not a tree. Practically: Layer 1 is the URL structure; Layer 2 is a field on the marker record. Never a nested folder, never a breadcrumb.

What it costs

Every marker needs two attributions instead of one, and both need clinical sign-off. It is the more expensive answer, and I think it is worth it — but you should know that before approving it.

One question decides whether this works at all: what are the six health category scores actually scored on? If they are computed per biological system, then Layer 2 is the scoring model and Layer 1 is only navigation — which is fine, but the score section has to be labelled honestly. If they are computed per health area, the two layers are genuinely independent. Appendix A §06 asks it properly. Nothing in Phase 4 can proceed on a guess.

02 / Hero

Klokken × Dagslys

The idea: an ordinary Copenhagen morning quietly turns into something worth knowing before the day is out. Time appears as a record — three marks on a hairline, sitting still, already there when you arrive. Never counting, never animating, never the headline.

The rule that keeps it warm. In all three executions the timestamps are the smallest type on screen, set in ink-3, below the fold of the headline, and never accompanied by a duration. 08.40 · 12.14 · 16.00 is a record of a day. "Svar på 4 timer" is an advertisement. The first is in; the second appears nowhere in the hero.

Image first, headline second — the reverse of a conventional hero. You are in the room before you are told anything. The time rule sits between them like a caption under a photograph, which is exactly what it is.

Typography

Where Nulpunkt lives

Three timestamps and two mono facts. Nothing else. It is 9.5px type in ink-3, below a 1px rule — present, precise, and impossible to mistake for a claim.

Mobile

The idea

Three fragments, three times, staggered indents. The timestamps are set as marginal annotations to the text — the way a footnote number sits beside a line in a book. Time is not a feature here; it is apparatus.

Typography

Serif D1 at 52px across three lines with indents at 0%, 6%, 2% — irregular on purpose, so the left edge reads as a considered ragged margin rather than a mistake. The subhead and CTA align to the text column, not the margin, which keeps the marginalia legible as marginalia.

"Fireogtredive"

Written out, not "34". At 52px in a serif it becomes a beautiful Danish word rather than a spec figure — and it removes the last trace of a stat line from the hero. The numeral survives everywhere else.

Mobile — genuinely different

Marginalia cannot survive at 390px, so the times move directly above each fragment. The photograph interrupts the headline, landing between fragment two and three. A magazine device, and it makes the mobile hero the more interesting of the two.

Motion

None at all. This is the only one of the three with no moving pixel anywhere.

Honest read

The most distinctive and the most demanding. It needs the serif to be excellent and the Danish to be set properly — fireogtredive must not break. Lowest photography risk of the three, because the image is supporting, not carrying.

The idea

One physical object photographed at three points on one day, hairline-separated, each with a time and a place. The third frame resolves into a Nulpunkt band — the tube literally becomes data in the first viewport. Home → laboratory → result, with no arrows and no diagram.

Why it isn't product advertising

The tube is never centred on white, never held up, never lit from two sides. It sits on a kitchen table in domestic light, then on a bench with a window behind it. The environment is the subject; the object is just what travels through it.

The laboratory, visible in the hero

Of the three, this is the only execution that shows Langebrogade above the fold. The mechanism behind the day is stated before it is claimed.

Mobile — two frames, not three

Three columns collapse to a 2-up: home and result. The laboratory frame moves into beat 06 where it has room to be a photograph rather than a thumbnail. A different composition, not a squeezed one.

Motion

The three frames are present at rest. The micro-band's tick slides in once, 500ms, after load. That is the entire budget.

Honest read

The most Nulpunkt-forward and the most explanatory. Risk: three frames plus a headline plus a band is a lot for a first viewport — it needs ruthless whitespace or it becomes a grid. Also the one that shows a blood tube in the first second, which is a real consideration for a squeamish visitor.

Comparison. V1 is the warmest and the safest, and rests entirely on one photograph. V2 is the most distinctive and the most typographically demanding — and the only one with zero photography risk. V3 explains the most and is the only one that puts the laboratory above the fold, at the cost of a busier first screen. All three carry the same three timestamps in the same register, so the Klokken idea survives whichever you pick.

03 / The day

The day sequence, refined

Pandonia's own sentence is the structure. Its tone is not.

The source

"Tag blodprøven om morgenen, modtag rapporten ved frokosttid, og få en lægekonsultation, inden du kører hjem fra arbejde."

Verified, from the results FAQ. The sequence is perfect — morning, midday, understanding, the rest of your day. The register is a scheduling instruction.

What changes

The imperatives go ("tag", "modtag", "få") — they make the reader the operator of a process. The rewrite puts the day in the third person and the past tense, so it reads as something observed rather than something you must execute.

What survives untouched

The four-part shape, and the closing idea that it is done before you go home. That last clause is the whole proposition in six words and it stays.

Daylight is the progress bar

The five photographs are graded from cold raking dawn to warm low afternoon. Nothing labels the passage of time except the light and five small numbers. It works only because the story genuinely fits in one day.

Density rises with the day

No numbers on screen until 09.05. One mono line at 09.30. The first Nulpunkt band at 12.14. Dagslys hands over to Nulpunkt in front of the reader, at the same pace the day does.

What I have not fabricated

The times are illustrative of a real published window (2–4 hours, morning draw, consultation ≥3 hours after results) and must be labelled as an example — or replaced with a real anonymised case. Appendix A §05 flags that 2–4 hours may be typical rather than guaranteed.

04 / The lab

The laboratory as mechanism

Not a trust badge at the bottom of the page. The thing the whole day is built around, present at three points and named.

Point one — the hero (V3 only)

Frame two is the laboratory, timestamped and captioned LANGEBROGADE. In V1 and V2 the street name appears once in the subhead instead.

Point two — beat 02

The facts line now reads EGET LABORATORIUM · KØBENHAVN where it previously read SVAR PÅ 2–4 TIMER. The mechanism replaces the consequence. §05.

Point three — beat 06, at 09.30

A photograph of the bench, and one line: "Vi laver den selv. Det er hele forskellen." It arrives mid-sequence, where the reader is already wondering how this is possible.

Point four — beat 09

Where it is finally explained: a named street, a named doctor, and the sentence that connects them. Held back until the reader cares.

How to photograph a laboratory without sterile stock

The rule

Shoot it like a workshop, not a cleanroom. A window in frame. Daylight, not overhead fluorescent. A hand labelling a tube. Depth of field so shallow that the room reads as warm shape rather than equipment. Worn surfaces.

Deliberately out of frame

Any wide shot of the room. Any full view of an analyser. Anybody in a full lab coat at a distance. Blue or green cast of any kind. Rows of identical instruments.

Why this matters

The lab has to add locality, not clinical coldness. A rack of tubes on a bench with a Copenhagen window behind it says "this is a real place three kilometres from your kitchen". A stock cleanroom says "this could be anywhere". The whole value of the fact is that it is here.

The claim to be careful with

Appendix A flags that accreditation and authorisation are unstated. Do not write "certificeret" or "akkrediteret" anywhere until Pandonia confirms it — the lab is the most scrutinised claim on the site precisely because it is the most valuable.

9 references, 5 of 11 beats. Speed appeared in the first line the visitor read, in the facts strip, in the process, in the trust section and in the closing line.

07 / Shot list

Photography brief

Eleven shots. One shoot day, one real Copenhagen apartment, one real draw, one visit to Langebrogade. Handable to a photographer as it stands.

Governing instruction: shoot it as if you were there and nobody asked you to come. Observed, not staged. If a frame looks arranged, it is wrong.

Global: natural light only, single source, no fill, no reflector, no flash. Lifted blacks, no crushed contrast, reds slightly desaturated so skin stays natural. No teal-orange grade. Wardrobe in cream, sand, oatmeal, olive, warm grey — no white shirts, no black, no logos, no blue. Casting: real Danish adults 30–65, no model casting, no styling beyond ordinary. Eyes to lens exactly once on the entire site (PORTRÆT-01).

08 / System

System changes

One reversal, one confirmation, one addition.

  1. Reversed: the hero clock does not animate

    In Revision A I proposed that if the hero carried the clock, the hero would animate and beat 06 would render complete. That was wrong, for a reason I missed: a record of something that already happened should not perform. A line that draws itself turns a timestamp into a countdown, which is precisely the stopwatch register we are avoiding.
    Settled: the hero's time rule is always static and present at rest. Beat 06 always carries the page's one orchestrated moment. This also restores the Phase 2 rule that the first viewport never animates.

  2. Confirmed: the hero need not be Dagslys-led

    All three executions carry photography, so no section is single-register and the §11 constraint holds. The Phase 2 wording changes from "the hero is photography-led" to "the hero is one valid place for either register to lead". V2 proves it — 55% Dagslys, 45% Nulpunkt, and it is the warmest-reading of the three on the page despite leading with type.

  3. Added, as you approved: no out-of-range values on the homepage

    The homepage demonstrates the Nulpunkt language with values inside their band. Reference ranges, out-of-range treatment and clinical interpretation live on Hvad vi måler and in the product. Brick is a clinical mark, never a marketing device. Now a standing rule, not a beat-level decision.

  4. Added: Layer 2 is always mono, always below a rule

    Wherever the biological model appears in the customer-facing site, it is set in DM Mono at 10–11px, below a hairline, under a label. It is never a heading, never a link target, never navigation. This is what keeps it reading as scientific context rather than a second menu.

09 / Decisions

Decisions before Phase 4

Four design decisions you can make now, and five facts only Pandonia can supply.

Yours to decide

  • 1 · Hero execution — V1, V2 or V3. Human, Editorial or Product. V2 is the only one with no photography dependency; V3 is the only one showing the laboratory above the fold; V1 is the warmest and the most conventional.

  • 2 · The two-layer taxonomy. Health areas navigate, the Pandonia model explains. Approving this commits to two attributions per marker and therefore to a longer clinical review. §01.

  • 3 · The speed audit. Nine time references down to four. If you think beat 02 still needs the hours, say so — I think the laboratory beats them, but it is a commercial call as much as a design one.

  • 4 · The photography budget. One shoot day, one apartment, one real draw, one lab visit, one portrait. Without it, V1 and V3 are not viable and V2 becomes the only honest choice.

Only Pandonia can answer — all of Phase 4 waits on these

  • 5 · The three missing health areas. Your concept gives Hjerte og kar, Blodsukker og stofskifte, Hormoner. Beat 07 currently renders three AFVENTER rows. Appendix A §01 lists eight health-area terms Pandonia already uses — evidence to choose from, not a proposal.

  • 6 · What the six scores are scored on. Health areas, biological systems, or a third thing. This single answer decides whether the two-layer model works.

  • 7 · The marker table. All ~34 with health area, biological system, unit, reference range, and whether it varies by sex or age. Every band on the site is a labelled placeholder until this exists.

  • 8 · Is it 30 or 34? Three figures currently in circulation. Appendix A §05 has the full discrepancy table — fifteen claims, eleven verified, seven open.

  • 9 · "Affaldssystem" / "detoxification", and "fordøjelse". Two of the six Layer 2 labels need clinical review before they go on a page. I have flagged both and deliberately proposed no replacement. Appendix A §03.

What I have not done, deliberately: named a single missing category, assigned a single biomarker to a single category, written a single reference range, or normalised "detoxification" into something more comfortable. Every one of those would have made this document look more finished and would have quietly turned a guess into a fact on a medical website.
