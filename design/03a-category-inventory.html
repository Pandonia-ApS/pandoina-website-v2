Throughout this revision I have not assigned a single biomarker to a category. Every mapping below is either quoted from Pandonia's own commercial copy or marked as unestablished. The line between "what Pandonia says" and "what would be reasonable" is kept visible on purpose.

01 / Two layers

Is there evidence for two layers?

Yes — and it is stronger than I expected. Pandonia already speaks in two vocabularies, in two different places, for two different purposes. Nobody has noticed because they never appear on the same screen.

Layer 2 — the biological model

Verified  Published once, in the FAQ answer to "Hvilke biomarkører analyserer I…", in both languages. It describes what the body does.

signalering · transport · fordøjelse · energi · immunforsvar · affaldssystem

This is explanatory language. It sits inside a sentence about how the body works, and it is the only place on the site where Pandonia describes a model rather than a product. It also matches the framing of the Pandonia Health Model — "a snapshot of your body's key systems".

It does not prove that any six of them are the six categories, and it assigns no biomarker to any of them. Assembling these into a set of six is a product decision, not a research finding. I have not done it.

Why the two layers are not a hierarchy

They cut across each other

Vitamin D appears in Pandonia's own copy under immune defence, bone strength and mental balance — three different concerns. Cholesterol is a "transport" question biologically and a "hjerte og kar" question clinically. This is a many-to-many relationship, not a tree.

Which means the IA can't nest them

You cannot put six biological systems inside six health areas, or the reverse. Layer 1 has to be the navigation, and Layer 2 has to be an attribute that a marker or an area carries — shown as context, never as a folder.

The good news

That constraint is exactly what the Phase 2 system is built for. Layer 1 becomes editorial structure — names, counts, photographs. Layer 2 becomes a mono annotation, which is already the register reserved for "this is how the instrument sees it".

03 / Review

"Affaldssystem" / "detoxification"

You asked me to flag this rather than silently normalise it. Here is the case, with no rewrite attached.

The two words do not mean the same thing

Affaldssystem is anatomically neutral and slightly industrial. It is not a standard term in Danish clinical language — you would normally say udskillelse, or name the organs. As a coinage it is at least honest and unglamorous.

Detoxification is a different proposition. In consumer health English it is the vocabulary of detox teas, juice cleanses and liver-flush supplements. It is arguably the single most compromised word in wellness marketing.

Why it matters more than the other five

Pandonia's whole credibility position rests on being the serious option — a real laboratory, named clinicians, a refusal to promise protection. "Detoxification" is the one word on the site that a medically literate reader would stop at. It undercuts precisely the audience most likely to pay 3.500 kr.

The Danish audience reads both languages. An EN/DK mismatch of this size is visible.

What I am not doing

Not proposing a replacement. If the markers behind this heading are liver and kidney function — which the marquee and the Metabolisk Sundheds Test copy both hint at — then several accurate names exist. Choosing one is a clinical and brand decision, and naming it here would quietly make it real.

What I would say: whatever it becomes, the DK and EN should describe the same thing.

The question for Pandonia

Is "detoxification" a deliberate translation choice, or an artefact of translating "affaldssystem" without a clinician in the room? The answer determines whether this is a copy fix or a taxonomy fix.

Second label worth the same scrutiny: "fordøjelse" / "digestion". Pandonia publishes no digestive or gut marker, and the booking system lists a gut product nowhere. If nothing in the 34 measures digestion, this heading describes something the test does not do — which is a more serious problem than an awkward word.

06 / Clinical

Taxonomy questions for a clinician

These are medical and product questions, not design questions. Nothing in Phase 4 can proceed on a guess at any of them.

  • Do the six biological systems have a defined marker membership? Is there an internal document mapping each of the 34 markers to signalering / transport / fordøjelse / energi / immunforsvar / affaldssystem — or is the six-system language descriptive rather than structural?

  • What are the six health category scores actually scored on? The six systems, a different set of user-facing areas, or something else entirely. This single answer determines whether Layer 1 and Layer 2 are two views of one model or two separate models.

  • Is the intended Layer 1 set the one in the concept? Hjerte og kar, Blodsukker og stofskifte, Hormoner — plus three more. If so, what are the other three, and who decided them?

  • Can a marker belong to more than one health area? Vitamin D plausibly touches immunity, bone and mood; cholesterol touches transport and cardiovascular. Function Health simply repeats a marker across categories. Whether Pandonia allows this changes the data model, not just the layout.

  • What do the panels contain? "Kolesterolprofil", "Elektrolytter og væskebalance", "Leverfunktion og proteinniveauer", "Nyrernes filtreringskapacitet", "Thyroid performance". Five named panels, zero published components. These may account for most of the 28 unrecovered markers.

  • Which reference ranges vary by sex or age? Two sex-specific products exist, but no sex-specific range is published. The Nulpunkt band has to state variation on the chart itself, so this is a build requirement rather than a nicety.

  • Which values are measured and which are derived? Drives the BEREGNET VÆRDI annotation. If, say, LDL is calculated rather than measured, saying so is worth more than any certification badge.

  • What does the test deliberately not cover? Function has no equivalent section and it would be a genuine differentiator. It needs a clinician to write it.
